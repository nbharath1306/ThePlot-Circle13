## Engagement Scripts

### Approaching Couples
**Script:**
"Hey! Have you seen ThePlot yet? It's an AI experience that simulates your relationship in 60 seconds. Both of you answer 3 questions, then watch what the AI predicts. Want to try?"

**Handling Objections:**
- "Is it accurate?" → "It's entertainment! But it's fun to see what AI thinks."
- "Is it safe?" → "Zero data storage. Everything disappears after 30 minutes."
- "Will it hurt our relationship?" → "It's just for fun! Plus 70% of couples 'make it' in the simulation."

### Post-Simulation
**Follow-up:**
"What did you think? Want to share your result on social?" 
*(Show phone for easy sharing)*

**Feedback Collection:**
"Quick question: On a scale of 1-10, how much did you enjoy that?"
*(Record responses for analytics)*

## Crisis Management

### Potential Issues & Responses

#### Issue: Couple has real fight over results
**Response:**
1. Intervene calmly
2. Remind: "This is AI entertainment, not real prediction"
3. Point to disclaimer: "See? It says it's not advice"
4. Offer mental health resources card
5. Document incident for review

#### Issue: Technical failure (site crashes)
**Response:**
1. Switch to backup device
2. Apologize: "Our AI just needs a coffee break"
3. Offer QR code for later: "Try tonight from home!"
4. Give out stickers/swag as apology

#### Issue: Negative press attention
**Response:**
1. Don't engage defensively
2. Point to safety measures: "We've prioritized ethics..."
3. Share positive testimonials
4. Offer interview with team lead
5. Transparency about limitations

#### Issue: Server overload
**Response:**
1. Implement queue system
2. "High demand! Grab a flyer, try tonight"
3. Collect emails for later access
4. Expand server capacity ASAP
